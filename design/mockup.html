The theme

Mosphit:The Final Verseমুখোশ

Inspired by Mukosh, a journey — it’s a lyrical war between the identity we show the world and the truth we bury within. Fueled by the raw energy of Desi hip-hop, hard-hitting bass, street-inspired flows, and poetry that cuts deep, every beat speaks of rebellion.

As the bass drops, the streets echo, we dive into a world of inner conflicts and broken identities. Through unfiltered lyrics, explosive rhythms the masks begin to crack, the silence turns into beat.

This isn't just hip-hop. This is the sound of the masks falling.

Who can attend?

Students from any college with a valid ID, plus anyone holding a pass. JGEC students get in with their college ID.

Do competitions need a pass?

Competition registration is separate from fest entry. Registered participants get a participant band for the days they compete.

Is there accommodation?

Outstation teams can request limited hostel accommodation when they register. Requests close on 10 December.

Can I transfer my pass?

Passes are non-refundable but can be transferred to another person until 15 December.

Demo answers. Final rules come from the organising committee.
